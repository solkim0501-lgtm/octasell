Overview

Fresh disinfectant. Every cycle.
Zero cross-infection.

OCTA-SELL electrolyses purified salt and water into sodium hypochlorite at the start of every cycle. The scope is cleaned under direct high-pressure water, disinfected and cleaned ultrasonically, triple-rinsed and air-dried. The solution is then discharged, so no batch of disinfectant is ever carried from one patient to the next.

Why OCTA-SELL

Conventional reprocessing reuses the same disinfectant

In a conventional AEWD the disinfectant sits in a tank and is used again and again before replacement, which raises questions about residual chemical carryover and cross-contamination depending on how it is managed.
